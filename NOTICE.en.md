## What actually protects the idea (not legal advice)

1. **Copyright** — arises automatically for code and texts; no registration needed.
2. **Publication with a date** — publish the repository publicly (GitHub) and/or obtain a
   DOI: Zenodo (free, issues a DOI), then OpenTimestamps/PGP-sign the commit.
   This fixes priority and makes the idea prior art.
3. **Deposit** — paid but inexpensive: deposit the work (e.g., with a copyright agency)
   or notarize the sources and the date.
4. **Patent** — algorithms as such are not patentable in the EU/RU; in the US a
   "computer-implemented process" is possible, but it is expensive and slow. If the idea is
   commercially valuable — go through a patent attorney. Cheap alternative: item 2.
5. **Trademark** on the name — if you plan a product.

Recommended minimum right now: fill in the author, publish publicly,
get a DOI on Zenodo, attach the hashes from this file.
